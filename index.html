Privacy Policy

BharatStream (package in.bharatstream.app)
Effective 24 October 2026
Published at https://shafiintj-source.github.io/bharatstream-privacy/

BharatStream is an independent all-India live TV and FM radio app published by BharatStream Media. This policy explains what the Android app version 3.0.6 does with information.

Advertising

Version 3.0.6 shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an interstitial when you leave a channel or switch channels, and an app-open ad when you return to the app. Google may collect and use your advertising ID, device identifiers, and approximate technical data (such as IP address) to show and measure ads. That data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive your name, phone number, email, contacts, or photos from AdMob. There is no in-app purchase and no account.

What we do not collect

This version does not ask you to create an account. It does not include Firebase or the Google Play Billing library. We do not receive your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location.

What stays on your device

Favourites and recently watched channels are stored only on your phone, in the app's local storage. You can clear them in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall BharatStream.

Playback

When you press Watch Live, the app opens a stream the broadcaster already publishes. That may be a YouTube page or a direct radio or TV URL. YouTube and the broadcaster then receive the usual connection data, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. We do not run those services and we do not get a copy of that data.

Some catalogue URLs use HTTPS. A few broadcaster URLs may use plain HTTP. Treat a live stream as content from that broadcaster, not from BharatStream.

Children

BharatStream is a general live TV and radio guide. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a later version adds accounts or payments, this page will be updated before that version is published.
