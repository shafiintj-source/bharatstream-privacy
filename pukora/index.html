Pukora – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pukora (“we”, “us” or “the app”) handles information when you use its social and communication features, including profiles, finding people, friend connections, text chat, image sharing and video calls.

1. Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, you may provide profile details such as a profile photo, date of birth and other information you choose to add. You may also send messages, images or other content, create friend connections, and participate in chats or video calls.

2. Camera and microphone

Pukora may request camera and microphone access when you use video-calling features. These permissions are used to enable the call. You can manage or revoke permissions in your device settings; video calling may not work without them.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate and maintain profiles, help users discover and connect with other users, provide chat and video-call features, send and receive shared content, maintain safety and security, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

4. Information visible to other users

Profile information and content you choose to share may be visible to other Pukora users according to the app’s features and settings. Messages and shared media are made available to the people or groups you send them to. Please do not share information you do not want other users to see or save.

5. Service providers and sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that support app hosting, storage, communications, security, analytics or advertising where those services are enabled. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the app or others. The providers and data involved depend on the services enabled in the version of the app you use.

6. Storage and retention

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the app’s features, maintain account functionality and meet applicable legal or security requirements. Retention periods can differ by information type and service provider.

7. Account and data deletion

You can use the account or profile options provided inside the app to request account deletion. Deletion of an account may not immediately remove information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reasons, or information already received and stored by other users.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children

Pukora is not intended to be used in a way that violates applicable child-safety or age-related laws. Users must not upload or share child sexual abuse material, exploitative content or other illegal content. Such content and related accounts may be reported and removed in accordance with applicable requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support/contact option provided in the Pukora app.
